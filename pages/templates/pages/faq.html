Frequently Asked Questions

- How extensive is your service's street coverage?
Our service covers all key areas and streets in the city, including both central and remote districts. We regularly update our data to provide accurate information across the entire city. If you need more specific details, you can use our search filters by district or street.
- What types of real estate can be found on your website?
Currently, our website only features listings for apartments for sale.
- How can I know if a property I'm interested in has been sold or unlisted?
We constantly update the information on our website. If a property is sold or removed from the market, we promptly update its status and remove it from available listings. You can always be sure that only up-to-date offers are displayed on our platform.
- Can I post my own listing on your website?
Unfortunately, you cannot post listings directly on our website as we are a real estate aggregator, not a direct listing platform. However, if you publish your listing on one of the popular real estate websites, it will most likely appear on our service as well.
